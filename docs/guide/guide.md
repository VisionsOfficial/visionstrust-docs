# Setup Guide

Everything you need is located across this documentation and the provided links to the Prometheus-X documentation. However, it can be quite daunting to get started with the amount of information at your disposal.

In an attempt to make this easier to adopt, this page is a step by step guide to everything you need to make your first data exchange.

## Non Technical Steps

1. [Register to {{ app_name }}](../application/onboarding.md)

If you are a Data or Service Provider

2. [Create an offer](../application/registering-resources/creating-an-offer.md)
3. Create a [data resource](../application/registering-resources/data-resources.md) or a [service resource](../application/registering-resources/service-resources.md)
4. [Publish your offer](../application/registering-resources/creating-an-offer.md#publishing-the-offer)

If you are a Project Leader

2. [Create a Project](../application/projects/creating-a-project.md)
3. [Configure your project](../application/projects/project-settings.md)
4. [Invite Data / Service providers to your project](../application/negotiation/invitations.md)
5. [Negotiate Data / Service offer usage for your project](../application/negotiation/project-negotiation.md)
6. [Publish your project](../application/projects/project-settings.md#understanding-project-publication)

## Technical Steps

> *You can also check the [requirements here](../guide/requirements.md) as additionnal information.*

7. [Install a PDC](../guide/setting-up-a-connector.md)
8. [Configure a PDC to connect to {{ app_name }} services](../guide/setting-up-a-connector.md#configuration)
9. [Ensure your PDC is connected to {{ app_name }}](../guide/setting-up-a-connector.md#checking-for-valid-configuration)
10. [Adjust the technical metadata of your offers](../guide/setting-up-technical-resources.md)
11. [Test your integration](../guide/testing.md)

### Optional: Consent for Personal Data

12. [Register to the Consent Service](../guide/setting-up-for-consent.md#registering-to-the-consent-service)
13. [Tag your resources as Personal Data](../guide/setting-up-for-consent.md#)
14. [Register users to the Consent Service](../guide/setting-up-for-consent.md#registering-users-to-the-consent-service)
